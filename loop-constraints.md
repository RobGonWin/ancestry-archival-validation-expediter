# AAVE loop constraints

1. Default to report-only and local execution; no autonomous publication or
   external write.
2. Require one objective, an explicit path allowlist, a sensitivity ceiling,
   evidence roles, verification commands, and stop conditions.
3. Never place raw DNA, genotypes, account captures, DNA-match identities or
   values, residence media, credentials, private vital records, or living-
   person identity data in prompts, logs, committed state, or graph labels.
4. Do not use a repository-root Graphify scan. Code mapping is allowlisted to
   reviewed source; non-code graph inputs must be public/synthetic or a
   separately sanitized opaque projection.
5. A family artifact, recollection, geographic community, and DNA context keep
   different evidence roles. None may silently become kinship proof.
6. In-law/marital edges never become biological edges. A longevity cluster
   grouping never becomes a genetic, cognitive, medical, or causal claim.
7. Preserve contradictions, uncertainty, null results, failed searches, and
   source conflicts.
8. Verify source licenses before reuse. Conceptual reference is not permission
   to copy unlicensed code or data.
9. Stop on boundary failure, unclear consent, missing provenance or license,
   sensitivity above ceiling, an undeclared external write, no measurable
   progress, the same failure three times, or three failed attempts.
10. A human reviews any privacy downgrade, public derivative, relationship
    conclusion, genetic interpretation, or material change to these rules.
