# Audit a research source

1. Prefer the primary publication, official repository, or current registry record.
2. Record authors, title, year, type, DOI or stable URL, access date, and immutable revision when available.
3. Distinguish primary observer reports, age-validation work, population studies, reviews, and secondary profiles.
4. Add `supports` and `cannot_support` statements in plain language.
5. Preserve conflicting dates, names, spellings, and later revalidation.
6. Treat population race categories as demographic variables, not DNA ancestry.
7. Require human review before connecting a public source to a private family claim.
