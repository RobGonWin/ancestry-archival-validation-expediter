# Private documentary reconciliation

Inventory only authorized local folders and exports; hash originals and keep extraction beneath ignored `local-vault`. Read existing GEDCOM people/family/source indexes before reparsing. Preserve vendor IDs and source pointers. Exclude genomes and unrelated living-person details from the slice.

For a photographed obituary, inspect the image, record its hash and narrowly transcribe the relationship or date statement. Mark what the document explicitly states; do not downgrade a legible statement to an unsupported recollection. Its documentary relationship remains distinct from genetic proof. Link matching tree relationships as a separate, potentially dependent source.

For census material retain the original-image reading, vendor transcription, enumeration date, reported age, place and household context separately. Flag spelling differences and uncertain handwriting. Reported age implies an interval, not an exact birth date. Do not silently normalize an index typo into the original text or infer absent household members.

Finish with a reconciliation table of claim, source hashes, documentary support, conflicts and limits. Keep family identities and excerpts private; a public summary may expose only separately authorized redacted findings. A census identity match or family relationship does not establish an award-wide longevity rank.
